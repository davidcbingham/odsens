METAL PIPE MACE

MOD
1.21.4 1.21.1 Fabric NeoForge 3,209 DOWNLOADS
SCREENSHOT 1 / 6
+2
ABOUT
The mace hits like a mace. The pipe sound plays on impact, at a volume I have decided is correct. There is no config file. There will not be a config file.
What's in it
One weapon, craftable with three iron ingots and a stick. Enchantable. Slightly too loud.
Works in multiplayer. Works with shields, badly, which is the intended experience.
NOTEUntested below 1.20. If it breaks, tell me in the comments and I'll probably fix it.

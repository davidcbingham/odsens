Pixel accent — micro-labels only. Eyebrows, download counts, badge text, section numbers. 10–12px, letter-spaced. Never a sentence.

05 — VOICE & TONE

DRY, SHORT, DEADPAN

odsens talks like someone who thinks the joke is funnier if you don't point at it. State the thing. Stop. The mods are silly; the writing about them is calm. Never hype, never “epic”, never exclamation stacking.

DO
“A mace made out of a metal pipe. It does the sound.”
“Works on 1.21. Probably works on 1.20. Untested.”
“Only on odsens. Not going anywhere else.”
“Sign in to comment. Your handle is all anyone sees.”
“Held for review. Usually quick.”
Rules: one idea per sentence. Under 12 words where you can. Lowercase in UI labels, caps only in Bungee. Own the limits — “untested” is funnier than a promise.
DON'T
“The ULTIMATE mace experience!!! 🔥🔥”
“Revolutionary gameplay-changing mechanics.”
“Hey guys, welcome back to my website!”
“Please consider supporting me, it really helps!!”
“An error has occurred. Code 500.”
No emoji, no hype adjectives, no vlogger openers, no begging on the support panel, no robot error text. Errors talk like a person: “That file didn't upload. Try again?”
MOTION & ACCESSIBILITY
Motion is light and blocky: 120–180ms, ease-out, 2–4px moves. Cards lift, shadows deepen, chips snap. One idle animation per page maximum (the hero's slow crown gleam). Nothing loops in the corner of your eye. Honour prefers-reduced-motion by killing transforms and keeping colour changes.
Every interactive thing gets a 44px target and a visible gold focus ring. Meaning never rides on colour alone — badges carry glyphs and words, statuses carry words, the active nav item carries an underline. Alt text on every project icon, screenshot and art piece. No real names, ages or locations anywhere: users appear as their chosen handle and picture only.
